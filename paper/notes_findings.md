
## Fabricated references propagating to arXiv (paper 147)
Submission 147 ("Mutual Wanting in Human-AI Interaction") cites at least five HCI papers that do not exist (Cross et al. 2024 CHI; Hur et al. 2024 CHI; Kaate et al. 2025a/b PACM HCI; Omeiza et al. 2025 NeurIPS). Google finds these titles only inside the paper's own OpenReview PDF and its arXiv version (Shang et al., posted 27 Oct 2025), i.e. the fabricated references were carried unchanged into the public preprint. Two further entries (Cheng et al. 2025; Fan et al. 2025) are real works with rewritten titles.
