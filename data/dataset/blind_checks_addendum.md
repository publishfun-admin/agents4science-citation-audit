# Blind-check addendum (round 3)

- Of the 180 blind-checked automatically verified entries, 37 came from accepted papers (35 EXISTS, 1 EXISTS_CORRUPTED, 1 UNADJUDICABLE, 0 NOT_FOUND), 109 from rejected (97 EXISTS, 10 EXISTS_CORRUPTED, 1 NOT_FOUND, 1 PLACEHOLDER), 22 from desk-rejected (14 EXISTS, 6 EXISTS_CORRUPTED, 1 NOT_FOUND, 1 PLACEHOLDER) and 12 from withdrawn submissions (12 EXISTS).
- Submission-clustered bootstrap (resampling the 125 submissions represented in the sample, 5,000 draws): corrupted 9.5% (95% 4.8-14.9), invented 1.1% (0.0-2.8).
- Expected share of reviewed submissions with at least one detected or undetected corrupted reference under the clustered draws: 84% (95% 72-92); a model expectation, not a measurement.
- Invented entries in the sample: 1 of 101 Crossref title matches and 1 of 5 "other" matches; the accepted-paper projection of undetected invented references (about 25, 8-57) applies these source-level rates to the accepted papers' verified entries and rests on two events.
